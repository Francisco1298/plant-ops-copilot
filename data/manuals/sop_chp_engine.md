# SOP-05 CHP engine operation

## Normal operation
The CHP engine converts biogas into electricity and heat. It needs methane above 55 % and H2S below 700 ppm.
Normal electrical output is about 220 kW.

## CHP engine trip
1. Check the alarm on the CHP panel: low gas pressure, high H2S or overheating are the most common causes.
2. Start the gas flare so the gas holder does not overpressure (see SOP-03).
3. Do not restart the engine more than twice in one hour without maintenance approval.
4. Record the trip time and the alarm code in the logbook.

## Low methane (below 55 %)
The engine loses power and may trip. Check digester temperature and feeding (see SOP-01 and SOP-02).
