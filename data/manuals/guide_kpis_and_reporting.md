# GUIDE-04 KPIs and daily reporting

## Key performance indicators
- Biogas production (Nm3/day): sum of the FT_201 flow. Typical value for this plant: about 2,880 Nm3/day.
- Methane content (%): target 58 % to 62 %. Below 55 % the CHP engine loses power and may trip.
- Electricity potential (kWh): methane volume x 9.97 kWh/Nm3 x 38 % CHP electrical efficiency.
- Specific yield: Nm3 of biogas per m3 of substrate fed. Typical value: 28 to 31.
- Data availability (%): share of valid sensor readings. Below 95 % the daily figures are not reliable.

## Daily status colours
- NORMAL: all KPIs within range.
- ALERTA: minor deviation (some hours out of range, low CH4 or low data availability).
- CRITICO: more than 2 hours of digester temperature out of range.

## Daily report to management
Send before 09:00: biogas, CH4 %, electricity, status and any event with its estimated impact in kWh.
Estimated impact = average production of NORMAL days minus production of the affected day.
