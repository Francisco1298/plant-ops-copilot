# SOP-02 pH and process biology

## Normal values
Digester pH (AT_102) must stay between 6.8 and 7.6. Normal value is around 7.2.
A falling pH means volatile fatty acids are accumulating: the digester is being overfed or the bacteria are inhibited.

## pH below 6.8 (acidification risk)
1. Stop feeding immediately. Overfeeding is the main cause of acidification.
2. Take a manual sample and measure FOS/TAC in the lab. FOS/TAC above 0.4 confirms instability.
3. Do not add chemicals without approval from the process engineer.
4. Restart feeding at 50 % of the normal rate only when pH is above 7.0 for 12 hours.

## Stuck pH sensor
If the pH value does not change at all for more than one hour, the sensor is probably stuck (frozen value).
Compare against a manual measurement. Clean the electrode and recalibrate with buffer solutions pH 4 and pH 7.
Report it to instrumentation maintenance. Data from a stuck sensor must not be used for decisions.
